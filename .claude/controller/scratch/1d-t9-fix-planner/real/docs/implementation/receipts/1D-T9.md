# 1D-T9: The conflict choice: 1D's ChoicePanel, the record's choice flow and its mark in History

- **Status:** built and verified locally on `p1/1d-t9-conflict-choice` (`.claude/worktrees/p1-1d-t9`), from `84ee167`. Not pushed. The independent review is the controller's, after this receipt.
- **What it does:** in the new `conflict` redesign area (off until E2, decided Q9), an online record edit refused with `VERSION_CONFLICT` is kept on this phone as a pending choice (1D-T4's diary) and shown on 1D's `ChoicePanel` (board `rh-conflict`): two radio cards, "Yours" chosen first, each differing field marked "Differs" in words and weight, one main button that follows the chosen card, and "Decide later" while the phone keeps the choice. Use yours resends the same changes on the server's current version under its own `choice:<id>` key; Use the server's drops the choice and sends nothing. Opening a record with a choice reads it first: a choice the server already holds clears itself, otherwise the panel compares with the fresh record (offline, with the stored one, and Use yours waits for the network). A deleted record offers Restore and Discard my change. History marks a record that waits with "Waiting for your choice". With the area off, today's comparison stays and nothing is kept. Record edits only (Q14).
