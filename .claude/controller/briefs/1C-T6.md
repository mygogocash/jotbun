## Planner rulings (the calls this brief makes, with the reason for each)

- **P1. What a fresh sign-in that does not go through returns.** The plan: deletion `denied`, export `reauth_required`, sign out everywhere `refused` with code `FRESH_SIGN_IN_FAILED`, nothing sent. Deletion's `denied` carries the same code, `FRESH_SIGN_IN_FAILED`; `deletion.ts` and `globalLogout.ts` each export `const FRESH_SIGN_IN_FAILED = 'FRESH_SIGN_IN_FAILED'` so the screens compare a name, not a literal. Why: one code for one cause across the three clients, and the screen must tell it apart from a server refusal (P3).
- **P2. A new session whose credentials cannot be read.** After `'fresh'`, `credentials()` (the provider's `requestCredentials()`) can answer null (the session changed during the read, or its token was refused: R86). Deletion and export then answer `reauth_required` and send nothing. A rejection of `freshSignIn()` or `credentials()` is not caught: nothing was sent, and the screen's catch shows today's sign-in line. Why: nothing can be sent without the new session's token, and "sign in again" is the only fix; it is also what 1C-T5's receipt asks Task 6 to do with an `unavailable` fresh sign-in.
- **P3. The screens keep their states and words.** A fresh sign-in that does not go through reads as today's "sign-in check expired" line on all three actions: Settings maps `refused` with `FRESH_SIGN_IN_FAILED` to "Your sign-in check expired. Nothing changed." (other refusals keep "Jotbun couldn't take your request right now. Try again later."); Privacy's delete maps `denied` with `FRESH_SIGN_IN_FAILED` to the idle `reauth_required` note ("Your sign-in check expired. Nothing was deleted.") and **never settles this phone's copy** (not erased, not locked: nothing was sent); export already shows that line for `reauth_required`. A `freshSignIn()` that throws keeps today's "Couldn't reach the sign-in service. Nothing ... Try again." Why: the plan says no copy changes; `settleDeviceCopy` locks the diary on any `denied`, which would lock a phone because a code was mistyped.
- **P4. Knowing what was sent.** Today the screens kept the proof a sign-in returned (`seen.proof`) to rebuild the pending request after a throw. Now they wrap the one resource call: Settings records that `signOutEverywhere` was called (`seen.sent`); Privacy records the request handed to `requestExport` or `requestDeletion` (`seen.request`, including its credentials). After a throw, a recorded request becomes `unconfirmed` with exactly that request (Try again resends it); none recorded means the sign-in failed and nothing was sent. Why: the plan's clients draw the ids and read the credentials inside, so the screen can no longer rebuild the request; wrapping the call records exactly what left the phone.
- **P5. Which build can run a fresh sign-in.** `freshSignInOf(provider: CredentialProvider | null)` in `SettingsParts.tsx` answers `() => provider.freshSignIn({ method, signUp: false })` for the account's own method, or null with no provider or account, for `email` (until Task 9 routes its code screen and sheet), and for a method not in `provider.methods()` (Apple and Google until Task 7 passes `appNativeSignIn()`). Both services call it once when the screen's services are made. Why: the plan's "null while the account's method has no fresh sign-in on this build (email until Task 9, Apple and Google until Task 7)"; with it, Task 7 turns Apple and Google on without touching these files. On today's build it is always null, so both screens keep their disabled reasons.
- **P6. The services.** `AccountServices.reauthenticate` becomes `freshSignIn: (() => Promise<FreshOutcome>) | null`; `sessions` is the port's sign out everywhere. `PrivacyServices` gains `freshSignIn`, `credentials(): Promise<RequestCredentials | null>` and `newId(): string` (the app: `getCredentialProvider()?.requestCredentials()` read at call time, and `Crypto.randomUUID()` from `expo-crypto`, already imported there). `signInMethod` stays null.
