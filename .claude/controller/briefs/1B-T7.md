## Planner rulings (the calls this brief makes, with the reason for each)

- **P1. The note reads its facts first (`ai.facts`), answers the plain facts without AI, and only with AI reads consent through `ai.preferences` (whose `companion` is `AiService.hasConsent`: a stored yes for the current notice), then its use.
