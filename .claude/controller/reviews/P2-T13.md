**m2. On iOS, a dismissal that completes after a quick reopen fires `onClosed` while the tray is open.** (behaviour lens BHV-4; PLAUSIBLE, because it depends on native timing)
- **Where:** `Tray.tsx:218` passes `onDismiss={Platform.OS === 'ios' ? () => closed.current?.() : undefined}`. Nothing checks that the tray is still closed.
- **How it can happen:**

  - `Modal.js:315-323` then calls `props.onDismiss` in a `setState` callback.
  - Both steps land after the tray's 220 ms hide.
  - If the tray reopens in that window (one to three frames), for example after a quick second tap on add, the late `onDismiss` calls the host's `onClosed` while `visible` is true.
- **Probe:** P-M2 in `behaviour/copy/apps/app/src/wallet/tray/iosDismissProbe.render.test.tsx`.
  - It wraps React Native's Modal so that `onDismiss` arrives late.
  - `onClosed` is called once while the tray is open again.
  - P-M1, the normal path, fires once.
  - Both reproduce in my rerun.
- **Why it matters:**
  - Brief P10(d) says a tray that opens again during its exit "says nothing".
  - P2-T16's `whenClosed(run)` runs from `onClosed`. It could open a queued second tray while the first is still open, which is the hazard that R69 exists to prevent.
  - Any other work a host does in `onClosed`, such as resetting the tray's state, would also hit the open tray.
- **Fix:**
  - Fire from `onDismiss` only while `visible` is still false (keep it in a ref).
  - Consider holding `shown` until `onDismiss` arrives, before the tray presents again.
- **Carrier:** P2-T16.

## What was checked and found right

### 1. Closing and the lock (P9, P13, R63, Review Focus 1)
- **One rule:** `requestClose` (`Tray.tsx:162-164`) serves:
  - close (`:272`);
  - the backdrop (`:235`);
  - `onAccessibilityEscape` (`:244`);
  - the Modal's `onRequestClose` (`:216`);
  - the swipe (`:190-191`).

  It asks only while `closable = visible && !locked` (`:160`).
- **Locked:**
  - Close is disabled: `aria-disabled`, `accessibilityState`, and dimmed to 0.4.
  - The backdrop, Escape and the swipe ask nothing.
  - A locked swipe past the line springs back. P-H shows this through Gesture Handler's own `jsEventHandler` (BEGAN, ACTIVE, an update, END) after a rerender to locked.
  - These mutants are killed:
    - B3: back bypasses the lock.
    - B6: the swipe skips its own `closable` check, so a locked swipe stays down.
- **Leaving:** during the exit, the backdrop and Escape ask nothing (P-L). Close, the escape and the swipe leave with the card.
- **Back belongs to the host.** It still works while locked, and close does not (P-N).
- **The cover:**
  - `LockCoverArt` replaces the backdrop and the card inside the Modal.
  - `LayoutAnimationConfig skipExiting` wraps exactly one child, the `KeyboardAvoidingView`. Reanimated's `setShouldAnimateExiting` requires that.
  - On a phone, Reanimated 4.7.1's `LayoutAnimationsProxy::startAnimationsRecursively` passes `shouldAnimate=false` down the removed subtree, and ends exits that are already running. So the card never shows over the cover, even in the middle of an exit.
  - On the web, the exit copy goes into the frame, which is removed with the subtree.
  - The planner's R55 (the cover drawn over a card that stays) is killed.
- **Under the cover:**
  - The content unmounts, and the host keeps its state.
  - The web layer loses its `aria-label`.
