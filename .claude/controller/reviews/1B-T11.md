**m1. An import purge in the same alarm leaves `prune_files` due at the vault clock, because `pruneSnapshots` runs before `pruneImports`.** (data lens D1)

- **Where:**

**m4. The file due-time reads now run after every write, and they scan receipts and every import head. In a large vault they cost more than 0003 saves.** (data lens D3)

- **The plans** (EXPLAIN on every statement those two functions run):

  - `importsDueAt`'s record subquery (`previews.ts:345`) walks every import head through the primary-key prefix, purged heads included. Purged heads are never deleted.

- The lens also timed `attachmentsDueAt` with an index on status: 0.66 ms. So that index alone does not help.
- **Probe:** `zz-review-data.test.ts:279` (outputs "probe due-reads plans" and "probe due-reads cost").

**n7. 1C-T11's `markDeleting` deletes the alarm directly. It must also clear `alarmAt`.** (alarm lens n5)
- **Where:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-jotbun-p1c-better-auth.md:1311` (Task 11): `markDeleting` ends with `await this.ctx.storage.deleteAlarm()`.
- **Why:** since this task, `alarmAt` must mirror `getAlarm()` (P9). Only the constructor, `erase()`, `alarm()` and `rearm` update it (`user-vault.ts:94`, `:109`, `:135`, `:173`).
- **Today it is harmless,** because a deleting vault never rearms:
  - `assertSession` refuses its calls;
  - the background methods skip it;

- **Fix:** `markDeleting` sets `alarmAt = null` next to its `deleteAlarm`. Or it calls `this.rearm(this.fileContext())`, which deletes the alarm of a vault that is not active and keeps `alarmAt` right.

**n8. 1B-T12: the stale-snapshot gap with the API narrows, but does not close.** (data lens D6)
- **The claims:** the 1B-T8 review said the difference "goes away" with m-1's fix. The receipt says a snapshot a week past its expiry is deleted "as before".
- **The API** keeps a succeeded snapshot job for 30 days after it finished (`gogospend_prune_finished_jobs`, `0015_privacy.sql:822-826`). It drops the manifest a week past expiry (`0010_sync.sql:224`).
- **So,** from about day 8 to day 30 after a build:

- **Probe D's residual** reproduces it (`data/probes-run1.log`): GET answers 404, then the resend answers 202 `pending` with the same id, and a fresh key gets "the same stuck id".
- **Fix, if parity is wanted:** keep the vault row until `ready_at` plus 30 days. That would match the API's answers in that window.
- **Carrier:** 1B-T12.
