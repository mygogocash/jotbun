## Findings

### Blockers

None.

### Major

**M1. The confetti decision bypasses the wallet's one reduced-motion answer (W6, R66).**
- **The code.** `WalletRecapScreen.tsx:58-59` reads `useWalletReducedMotion()` and `useReducedMotionKnown()`, and never uses them. Instead, the effect at `:69-88` calls `AccessibilityInfo.isReduceMotionEnabled()` itself and races it against `setTimeout(..., 0)` (`:79-82`). Confetti plays only if the phone's reply wins the race.
- **What the rules say:**
  - W6: the reduced-motion answer is asked once, in `WalletRoot`, and reads as reduced until the phone answers.
  - `walletMotion.ts:73` and the plan say the recap reads that answer and decides at mount.
- **Why the suite passes.** `mountWallet` replaces `isReduceMotionEnabled` with an already-resolved promise, so the reply always wins.
- **On a phone.** The reply is a native callback (`NativeAccessibilityManagerIOS.getCurrentReduceMotionState`), while RN fires one-off 0 ms timers at once (`RCTTiming.mm:363`). Confetti can therefore fail to play at full motion on a device.
- **Evidence.**
  - PROBE-A1: the wallet answer is known as full motion and the phone replies in 5 ms; no confetti.
  - PROBE-A2: the recap asks the phone a second time.
- **Fix.** Decide at mount from the wallet's answer (`motionFor('recap', reduced)`, with pending reading as reduced). Then delete the race.

**M2. A share that cannot open is unhandled and silent.**
- **The code.** `handleShare` (`WalletRecapScreen.tsx:149-162`) awaits `shareText` with no catch. `PrimaryButton` (`tray/PrimaryButton.tsx:38`) hands `onPress` to `Pressable`, which drops the promise.
- **What the plan says.** It names the rejection: `shareText ... rejects when the sheet cannot open`. The Bun diary recap keeps `shareProblem` around it ("เปิดการแชร์ไม่ได้ ลองอีกครั้ง").
- **When it happens.** react-native-web's `Share.share` rejects wherever `navigator.share` is missing (desktop browsers), and `navigator.share` rejects when the person cancels. In those cases the wallet recap shows nothing, and the rejection is unhandled.
- **Evidence.** PROBE-B: no problem line, and Vitest reports `Unhandled Rejection: Share is not supported in this browser`.
- **Fix.** Catch the rejection and show the same catalog line, `tc("Couldn't open sharing. Try again.")`.

**M3. The cashback card opens GoGoCash, and ignores the bridge's off switch and A5's rule.**
- **R73.** The card (`WalletRecapScreen.tsx:268-284`) is a `link` that calls `Linking.openURL(GOGOCASH_HOME)` (`:272`).
  - W13 and R73 say phase 2's cashback count opens nothing.
  - P6B's X3 says its cashback tab is "opened by the pill, tile and recap card (ending R73's 'opens nothing')". So the recap card's opening belongs to P6B.
  - PROBE-F1 confirms the link opens.
- **The bridge's off switch.** The count uses `BUILTIN_CASHBACK_BRANDS` and the constant URL (`:145-147`). The approved card (`screens/redesign/insights/CashbackCard.tsx:25-39`) reads `cashbackCatalogue()` and shows nothing while the bridge is off on the server (404). PROBE-F2 shows the recap still showing the card then.
- **The brands line.** The detail line is fixed: "จาก Shopee และ Lazada" (`copy.ts:21`). It reads the same for an Agoda or TikTok Shop month (PROBE-G). `countCashbackPurchases(...).brands` holds the shops that actually matched.
- **A5's rule.** The plan calls `countCashbackPurchases` and `cashbackView` A5 (line 1264; P2-T24's pill shows "A5's count when `cashbackView` says tips"). The recap tests only `preferences?.cashback_tips`, so it skips `cashbackView`'s pending answer and its 7-logged-days floor (`cashbackModel.ts:37-40`). The controller may want to confirm that "only when tips are on" means `cashbackView === 'tips'` here.

**M4. The four catalogue sections are hand-drawn mocks, not `WalletRecapScreen` on fakes.**
- **The code.** `recapSections.tsx:45-150` draws `MockRecapCard`, fixed Thai strings and literal colours. It never draws the screen.
- **Why that matters.** E2-C is approved from screenshots of these sections, one deep link each. The registry's rule (`registry.tsx:10-12`) and the catalogue suite's test "screen sections draw the real screens on today pinned to the fixtures" say a screen state is drawn by the real screen on fakes, as `insights/recapSections.tsx` does for the Bun diary recap.
- **Consequences:**
  - The owner would approve a card with no close button, month chip, top three, cashback logic or share button (PROBE-H1).
  - `bw-recap-private` shows ฿2,380 in private mode, because its biggest-day line is a plain string at `:49` (PROBE-H2).
  - Every language shows Thai (PROBE-H3).
  - The sections are in group `'Kit'`, not `'Screens'` with `layout: 'screen'`.
- **CI.** CI's per-section runs pass because they check only that a section renders without console errors. They do not check what it draws.

**M5. `biggestDay` and the cashback count read records from a reader built to stop early.**
- **The code.** `recapData.ts:36-41` collects records inside the page reader given to `readMonthDays`. That reader stops once every day of the month has been seen, or after 20 pages (`recapModel.ts:76-92`, line 88).
- **What goes wrong.** History arrives `date_desc`. In a month with every day logged and more than one 200-entry page, the rest of the earliest day's entries are never read.
- **Evidence.** PROBE-C, with rent on the 1st and 204 entries, shows the biggest day as 2 Sep, ฿600. It should be 1 Sep, ฿8,290 (the summary's ฿25,690 is right). The cashback count is short the same way.
- **Fix.** Read every page for the records (`readAllPages`, `insightsModel.ts:250`), separately from the days' early stop.

**M6. Colour and type bypass the wallet kit, including a dark-mode contrast failure.**
- **Contrast.** The left-over amount overrides the `under` token with light mode's `#1A7F4B` (`WalletRecapScreen.tsx:220`). That colour also shows in dark mode, where it reads 3.47:1 on the dark card `#1A1920` (PROBE-D). The Global Constraint is 4.5:1 for text; dark `under` is `#4CC38A`. `tone="under"` alone is right.
- **Hex literals.** The recap files hold the only hex literals in `src/wallet` outside `BunFace.tsx`: 8 in the screen (`:220, 276, 282, 395, 402, 413, 423, 428`) and 5 in `Confetti.tsx:8`. Plan line 1777 (P2-T37's sweep) fails on any literal outside `walletPalette.ts` and `BunFace.tsx`, Confetti included ("Confetti uses palette values").
- **Dark mode.** The cashback card is `#111014`, so it vanishes into dark ground `#0E0D12`.
- **Type.** Every line is raw `Text` with fixed sizes, not `WalletText` roles. As a result:
  - there is no Anuphan (Global Constraint "Type, §5.1, R74");
  - there is no Thai leading;
  - there is no AX5 cap (`maxFontSizeMultiplier`, W30).

  The Thai month chip is set in DM Sans (`:328-331`), though DM Sans is for amounts only. PROBE-Y shows the title and days with no font family, and the chip as `"DM Sans", Anuphan`.

**M7. The loading and error states speak English in Thai and leave no way out.**
- **The strings.** `tc('Loading…')` (`:95`) and `tc('Something went wrong')` (`:103`) are in neither `en.json` nor `th.json`, so Thai shows English.
  - The suite asserts the English line under `locale: 'th'` (`WalletRecap.render.test.tsx:187,192`).
  - P2-T37's sweep ("no wallet screen calls tc() with a string missing from en.json") will fail on both.
- **No way out.** The error state has no Try again and no offline wording (PROBE-E). The Bun diary recap uses `recapProblem` with a retry.
- **Fix.** Reuse catalog lines, or add draft lines to `copy.ts`. Draw `recapProblem(load.error, tc)` with `load.reload`.

- **The top three.** "The recap draws bw-recap's facts" never reaches them, because the fake summary always returns `categories: []` (`fakeResources.ts:255`). The empty-month case asserts the absence of 'หมวดหมู่ที่ใช้จ่ายสูงสุด' (`:175`), a string the screen never draws. O4 and O5 survive.
- **Over and left.**

  - The share text's over and left words are untested (O3).
- **Ties in `biggestDay`.** The tie is tested only with records in date order, never in history's `date_desc` order (O1). Transfers are never in the fixture (O2).
- **Analytics.**
  - The events are `vi.mock`ed instead of using `recordAnalytics()`, with its "exactly one" and "no amount, note or name in `JSON.stringify(sent)`" checks (Global Constraint "Analytics").
  - The failed-share path is untested (O6).

### Minor

**m1. The top three fork `recapModel.topCategories`, and the screen copies `dateStep`'s month names.**
- **Top three.** `WalletRecapScreen.tsx:126-142` re-sorts the summary with no tie rule. The model sorts ties by name, then id (`recapModel.ts:107-122`), so equal totals come out in a different order (PROBE-T2). Plan: "the pure models stay the source of every number". Use `topCategories(..., 3)`.
- **Dates.**
  - `formatShortDate` and its two month tables (`:39-49`) copy `dateStep.ts`'s `monthsShort`; Vietnamese gets English months there and in `monthLabel(month, locale === 'th' ? 'th' : 'en')` (`:90`). Global Constraint "Time": dates on screen go through `src/ui/dateStep.ts`.
  - `daysInMonth` (`:32-37`) copies `redesign/shared.ts`'s.

**m2. A failed or loading first open spends the month's one celebration.**
- **The code.** The effect plays the haptic and keeps `CONFETTI_NAME(month)` at mount, whatever the load does.
- **What goes wrong.** An offline first open plays `celebration` over the error state and keeps the mark (PROBE-N). That month's recap then never celebrates once it loads.
- **Fix.** Decide once the data is ready.

**m3. Close calls `router.back()` (`:175`) with no fallback.**
- **What goes wrong.** A recap opened from a link or a notification has nothing to go back to: expo-router reports an unhandled `GO_BACK`, and the button does nothing.
- **Fix.** The Bun diary recap uses `useGoBack('/spend/insights')`.

**m4. There is no not-started or empty state.**
- **What goes wrong.** A hand-typed future month, which the route accepts, draws a ฿0 recap with 0 days and a live share button. An empty past month gets the same, with confetti.
- **Compare.** The Bun diary recap has both states.

**m5. Accessibility details.**
- **Headings.** The title and "หมวดที่ใช้มากสุด" are not headings; the board's title is an `h1`.
- **Decorative glyphs.** The card's "✦" and "›" are read aloud: the `Pressable` has no label, and the glyphs are not hidden.

**m6. The receipt does not match the code or the logs.**
- **It describes Confetti wrongly.** It "uses Reanimated layout / animations": the pieces are static Views. It keeps "`jotbun.wallet.confetti-seen-{month}`": the key is `jotbun.wallet.recap-confetti-{month}`.
- **Its line counts are the brief's, not measured.** For example, the screen is 434 lines, not 320; `recapSections.tsx` is 151, not 70; `Confetti.tsx` is 66, not 78.
- **Some gate lines have no log in `scratch/p2-t32/`.** These are `typecheck:routes`, `test:app` 34, `test:unit` 978 and `git diff --check`. CI's checks job is still queued.
- **"Deviations" lists only the planner's calls, not these interface changes:**
  - `biggestDay`'s third parameter;
  - `useRecapData` returning `RecapLoadWithRecords`;
  - `Confetti` taking props;
  - the extra copy keys (they are the board's, which is fine; `recap.sister` is unused);
  - the mock sections;
  - the GoGoCash link.

**m7. `biggestDay`'s `'salary'` fallbacks are dead in production.**
- **The code.** It falls back to `'salary'` when no salary category is found (`biggestDay.ts:21`), and also matches a literal `category_id === 'salary'` (`:35`).
- **Why that is wrong.** Category ids are UUIDs, so these only serve hand-made fixtures. A missing salary category would quietly match nothing.
