## What holds

### 1. `AiService`'s order and refusals (P1, P10): holds, m-1 aside

- **The reference:** `apps/api` has no AI service. The service lens compared the code line by line with the PostgreSQL edge's `AiService` (`apps/edge/src/server/ai/service.ts`) and `test/ai.db.test.ts`. The 17 cases are ported by name and assertion, and each P11 change has its comment.
- **Tell Bun, slips and Ask Bun.** The order is:
  1. `ai.context`, where consent (403) comes before the lists;
  2. the switch: Tell Bun answers from the rules, and slips and Ask Bun answer 503 `AI_PAUSED` with a retry of 300 seconds;
  3. `ai.consume`;
  4. the model.
- **The note.** The order is:
  1. `ai.facts`, which gives the plain facts without AI;
  2. `ai.preferences` (consent), only with AI;
  3. `ai.consume`, inside the `try`;
  4. the model.

  A caller sees the same order as in `AiService` on every route.
- **`consumeAi` counts before the model** (`src/vault/ai.ts:95-107`).
  - Its 429 `AI_LIMIT_REACHED` is thrown inside the call's `transactionSync`, so the increment and the 35-day delete both roll back (`user-vault.ts:135-160`).
  - The SQL means what `AiService`'s did: `date(?, '-35 days')`, `uses > limit`, and a retry at Bangkok midnight, all on the vault clock.
- **Preferences:** `setAiPreferences` writes and then answers `aiPreferences(c)` in the same step's transaction. Its 403 carries `errors.ai_notice_required`, and the service adds `available` (PROBE-10).
