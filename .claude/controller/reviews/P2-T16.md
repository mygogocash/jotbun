### Major

**M1. Private mode: a conflict draws and says a stored amount in digits (Review Focus 2; W10; R106; the controller's private-mode focus).**
- **How it happens:**
  - The host shows the save's problem from `entrySave.message`. The text is read at `AddTrayHost.tsx:284`, drawn at `:396-400` as `add-save-problem`, and said on iOS at `:285`.
  - For a conflict, `useEntrySave` builds that message from the server's held record: `words(held.amount_minor).shown` goes into "This entry is already saved in Jotbun as {amount}. Check it in History." (`entrySave.ts:126-127`).
  - `words` is `useAmountWords()` (`ui/Amount.tsx:70`), which does not know about the wallet's private mode.
- **PROBE-5** (`probes-1.log`): private mode on, online, a lost answer, then Try again finds the record held with `amount_minor: '9900'`.
  - On screen: `รายการนี้บันทึกไว้ใน Jotbun แล้ว ยอด ฿99.00 ตรวจสอบได้ในประวัติ`.
  - In the iOS announcement: the same words.
- **What leaks:**
  - The footer shows a stored amount in private mode.
  - The person's amount may be a fill (Bun's, a chip's or a copy's), which the same screen masks as ฿•••• (R106). The held amount then reveals that fill too, unless the record was changed elsewhere.
- **How likely:**
  - The path is narrow: an online save whose answer was lost, then a resend that finds other details.
  - But it is one of the tray's planned states (the plan: "`unconfirmed` and `conflict` show today's message"), and this task's own test drives it (`AddTray.render.test.tsx:419-435`, with private mode off).
- **Nothing later catches it.**
  - P2-T37's sweep reads each section's text and labels.
  - No section draws a conflict, and an announcement is neither text nor a label.
- **Fix (needs a ruling, as R114 did):**
  - **The smallest fix is in this task's file.**
    - When private mode is on and the status is `conflict`, draw and say the conflict words without the amount.
    - The words: a draft line in `add/copy.ts` (Thai first, a draft for E2-B), or today's line with `common.amountHidden` as `{amount}`.
  - **Pin it:**
    - PROBE-5's shape: no digit in `add-save-problem` or in the announcement;
    - a mutant that drops the private-mode branch.
  - **The alternative:** a private-mode option on `useEntrySave`. That hook is P2-T5's file, shared with Quick add.

### Minor

**m1. On iOS, a tray that never presented wedges every later open until the app restarts (P5d; the controller's question).**
- **Answer:** yes, the queue can wedge every later open and every `whenClosed` run. A person cannot get out inside the app: only a restart helps, or anything else that remounts the host.
- **Evidence:**
  - **React Native 0.88's iOS Modal** (`node_modules/react-native/React/Fabric/Mounting/ComponentViews/Modal/RCTModalHostViewComponentView.mm:164-201`).
    - It sets `_isPresented = YES` (`:172`) before it asks UIKit to present.
    - `onShow` fires only from the present's completion (`:175-179`), and `onDismiss` only from the dismissal's completion (`:195-199`).
    - If UIKit refuses the present (another controller is presenting or being dismissed), neither block runs for that present.
    - Whether a later `dismissViewControllerAnimated` on a controller that was never presented calls its completion is the open device question (P5d, P19).
  - **The host relies on `onClosed` alone.**
    - The host's side is `AddTrayHost.tsx:174-177`.
    - In `Tray.tsx`, on iOS only `onDismiss` reports the close (`:158-166`, `:175-177`).
  - **PROBE-W** (`probe-wedge.log`): a Modal that never sends `onDismiss`, on iOS. After a close and 2 s:
    - `open({ type: 'income' })` has drawn nothing, and `isOpen` is false;
    - a `whenClosed` run has not run;
    - only the first opening's `record_started` was sent.
- **Two ways in:**
  - **The present is refused at open.** The host holds `visible: true` with nothing on screen. Every later `open` is ignored outright (`:154-157`; it is not even queued), and there is no tray to close.
  - **No dismissal arrives after a close.** The entry never clears, so every `open` and every `whenClosed` run (such as P2-T30's) waits for ever.
- **When it can happen.** The host orders only its own trays, so any other Modal presenting or leaving at the moment of an open can trigger it:
  - P2-T30's over-budget tray: a queued add-tray open and P2-T30's queued run both run in the same `closed()` loop, so two Modals present in one frame;
  - the lock's screen;
  - a sheet.
- **The brief records this as a risk with a device check, and P2-T18 makes it reachable** (the + button, Magic Tap). Settle it before P2-T18 ships. Options:
  - on iOS, a tray that never got `onShow` treats its hide as closed at once;
  - the host clears an entry after a bounded wait;
  - one coordinator for every wallet Modal.
- `Tray.tsx` is not this task's file, so this belongs to a tray follow-up, not a fix round.

**m2. The lock's cover loses what the person typed into Tell Bun (R63; `followups.md` line 509, "Later Sheet hosts").**

   - **Locked only while saving:** `locked={inFlight}` (`:430`). My R7 (`locked={locked}`) is killed: an unconfirmed or conflicted tray can still be closed.

     - My R6 (the flag never clears after a save) is killed by the conflict case's Done.

   - **The refused save's words:** drawn above Save, as an alert that iOS also announces (H28 to H30). Their order is unpinned (n1).
