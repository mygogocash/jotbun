### Blocker

### Major

**M1. Private mode: an amount written in words in Bun's sentence reaches the screen (P8c; Review Focus 2; the controller's focus 4).**
- `ConfirmStep.tsx:60` masks `/(?:฿\s?)?[0-9๐-๙]+(?:[.,][0-9๐-๙]+)*/g`, which matches ASCII and Thai digits only (`:92`).
- Bun reads more than digits. Its parse prompt says "Thai number words such as ห้าสิบ mean 50" (`apps/edge/src/server/ai/prompts.ts:14`). So the amount Bun reads from "ข้าวมันไก่ห้าสิบบาท" fills the keypad as a fill, and `confirm-amount` is masked. The sentence beside it is not.
- Probes, with private mode on and a fill of ฿50 (`probes-run2.log`):
  - PROBE-1a, Thai: `confirm-amount` is `฿••••`, and `confirm-bun` is `บันอ่านจาก “ข้าวมันไก่ห้าสิบบาท”`. Fails "not to contain ห้าสิบ".
  - PROBE-1b, English: `confirm-bun` is `Bun read this from “chicken rice fifty baht”`. Fails.
  - PROBE-1c, full-width digits: `confirm-bun` is `บันอ่านจาก “ข้าวมันไก่ ５０”`. Fails.
  - The line is visible text, so a screen reader reads it as well.
- P8c was written to stop exactly this ("it holds the amount Bun read, beside an amount masked as a fill"). The controller's check also says "no amount or number in Bun's sentence reaches the screen ... in Thai and English". The 14 cases test digits only (`AddSteps.render.test.tsx:483-486`).
- **Ask:**
  - In private mode, quote none of the sentence: for example `t('add.bunReadFrom', { sentence: '••••' })`, or leave out the quote.
  - A word list cannot be complete. Thai writes numbers without spaces and in many forms (ห้าสิบ, ร้อยนึง, สองพันห้า, ห้าร้อยกว่า), and so does English (fifty, a hundred, two fifty).
  - Add a case with Thai number words, English number words and full-width digits, and keep the digit case.
  - If the planner wants to keep part of the sentence in private mode, the controller rules on it. As it stands, the mask leaks.

### Minor

**m1. A side whose wallet is archived asks for one, but Next still goes on, and confirm then names that wallet (P4e, P6).**
- `TransferStep.tsx:97` hides an archived or unknown wallet (`listed`), so its card asks for one (เลือกกระเป๋า). But `TransferFooter` (`:174-176`, `:195`) checks only the draft's ids, so Next stays enabled.
- PROBE-2, with `fromWalletId: 'w-old'` (archived):
  - the card says `จาก เลือกกระเป๋า`, and Next has no `aria-disabled`;
  - after Next, the confirm rows read `จาก กระเป๋าเก่า`, `ไป เงินสด`, and so on.
- P6 says "a side with no wallet only keeps Next waiting", and the card has just told the person that this side has none. `checkEntryFields` does not check for archived wallets either, so the save would go on to the server.
- The case for it (`AddSteps.render.test.tsx:449-451`) checks the card and move all, but not Next.
- This is latent today. The add flow's drafts cannot hold an archived id:
  - `duplicateDraft` nulls closed wallets (`recordModel.ts:152`);
  - `newQuickAddDraft` takes only the wallets in use;
  - `useEntryReferences` keeps its lists for the form's life.

  A host that passes an archived id, such as a later edit or copy path, would reach it.
- **Ask:** decide Next by the card's rule. Either give `TransferFooter` the wallets, or have the step treat an archived or unknown side as none in both places. Then add Next's state to the archived case. Alternatively, record in P18 that the host must null an archived side before the transfer step, and pin that in P2-T16.

**m2. No case holds `isPickStep`, or the way back from the amount chip's wallet pick (P3d; the controller's focus 3).**
- The planner's only mutant on `isPickStep` is P1 (nothing is a pick). Each of my mutants below survives all 14 cases:
  - R1 leaves `'wallet'` out of `PICKS` (`PickSteps.tsx:63`). The host then no longer records which step opened the wallet pick. So the amount chip's pick goes back to a stale step (confirm, which skips the amount step's Next check), and the tray's back disappears from that pick.
  - R2 leaves `'to'` out. The case still passes because it inherits `back` from the earlier `from` pick.
  - R3 counts `'confirm'` and `'transfer'` as picks. Confirm and the transfer step then gain a back to the last pick.
- The behaviour is right at `fd41909c`. PROBE-5 drives P2-T14's real `AmountStep` chip (`add-wallet`) to the wallet pick and picks เงินสด. It lands back on the amount step with the chip reading จากเงินสด, and the tray's back also returns there. PROBE-5 kills R1; the suite does not.
- **Ask:** add one case that opens the wallet pick from the amount step's chip and comes back by a pick and by the tray's back. Add a table check of `isPickStep`: the six picks true; amount, transfer and confirm false.

**m3. Three accessibility behaviours that the brief names are not held by any case.**

P2-T14 pinned the same call in `AmountStep` with a source test (`amountStepNative.test.ts`) after its reviews (the first review's M2, under m5; the second review's n2).
