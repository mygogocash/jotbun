### Nits

**n1. 1B-T13 should keep `default_retention` on both Workflows bindings and pin it in `c1-shape.test.ts`.** (lens A) Carrier: 1B-T13.
- The guarantee in n2 (a resend after day 8 can start a new build) rests on `apps/edge/wrangler.vault.jsonc:35` to `:36`: `default_retention` of 1 day on success and 7 days on error, on both bindings. Plan 1B-T13 moves the bindings as "both Workflows" (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-jotbun-p1b-vault-files.md:1117`, no retention named), and its shape test checks the Workflows' binding names only (`:1168`).
- Without the setting the default is the account's maximum, 30 days on the Paid plan (Workflows docs, Context7 `/websites/developers_cloudflare_workflows`, the `WorkflowInstanceCreateOptions` notes; read here too). `createOnce` (`apps/edge/src/vault/files/work.ts:45-55`) counts a `create` that throws for a retained id, followed by a `get(id)` that resolves, as started. A resend in days 8 to 30 would then leave a pending row with no running build, and `pruneSnapshots` fails it a day later (`snapshots.ts:345`).
- Fix: keep both `default_retention` blocks in the merged `wrangler.jsonc` and assert them in `c1-shape.test.ts`.

**n2. P3 (the n8 difference) is described correctly, but its citations omit what starts day 8, and its open question (d) can be closed.** (lens A; code citations checked here) Carrier: snapshot-parity-window.
- The text in the receipt and in the header of `vault-parity-files.db.test.ts` (`:12` to `:17`) matches the code. NestJS: a succeeded snapshot job with no manifest reads `expired` (`apps/api/src/sync/snapshot.repository.ts:63-65`), and a resend of the key returns that old job's id with 202 (`sync.controller.ts:57`; `gogospend_request_snapshot` returns the existing job, `0010_sync.sql:88-93`). The job row is pruned 30 days after it finished (`0015_privacy.sql:815-830`). Vault: a ready row is deleted a week past its expiry (`apps/edge/src/vault/files/snapshots.ts:41`, `:348`), so the status is 404 and a resend inserts a pending row (`snapshots.ts:90-93`).
- Missing from P3: what sets day 8 on the NestJS side is the manifest sweep `gogospend_prune_expired_manifests` (`0010_sync.sql:216-226`, `expires_at < now() - interval '7 days'`), not the 30-day job retention P3 cites (that gives day 30). Cite both.
- P3(d), "whether the vault can start a Workflow instance under an id whose old instance has passed its retention", can be closed from the repo's own config and the docs: `create` throws only for an id whose instance has not passed its retention (Workflows docs, read here), and `wrangler.vault.jsonc:35-36` sets that retention to 1 day and 7 days. So after day 8 a resend on the vault starts a new build under the same id. The 1B-T11 review's n8 wording "stuck pending row" needs the old instance to still exist, which holds for the first day only (local Miniflare retention behaviour not checked). The difference stays one of answer shape (404 and a fresh pending row against `expired`), not a stuck build, as long as n1 holds.

**n3. A second time-dependent difference is not named next to n8: pending work older than a day turns `failed` on the vault, never on NestJS.** (lens A; citations checked here) Carrier: snapshot-parity-window, or a follow-up.
- Vault: a pending snapshot created a day ago fails (`snapshots.ts:39` `SNAPSHOT_PENDING_MS`, `:345`), and a pending export requested a day ago fails (`exports.ts:55`, `:330`; plan ruling R33, plan line 672). NestJS has no wall-clock rule: `gogospend_request_snapshot` queues the job with `max_attempts` 3 and `max_runtime_seconds` 600 (`0010_sync.sql:109`), and the status stays `pending` while the job is queued or leased (`snapshot.repository.ts:60-62`; `apps/api/src/exports/export.service.ts:151`).
- Reachable only when background work stalls for a day, so the seconds-long session cannot show it. It is a designed rule, not a defect. Name it so that a day-old `pending` against `failed` is not taken for a bug when the two are compared later.
- Checked and equal on both sides, so not differences: upload file names cleared at 8 days and rows dropped 30 days after they ended (`intake.ts:29-31`, `:422-426` = `0014_import_intake.sql:569-570` plus `0015_privacy.sql:855-871`); export rows purged 30 days after the request, with a purge group (`exports.ts:57`, `:307-336` = `0015_privacy.sql:716-756`); preview 7 days; receipt digests dropped at 30 days (`retention.ts:37` = `0015_privacy.sql:758-771`); receipt copy grace 180 s and source margin 2 minutes; released receipt rows 30 days (`attachments.ts:31-39` = `0017_attachments.sql:446`); a parsed import's status after its parse job is pruned (NestJS falls back to the preview row, `0016_import_candidates.sql:396-405`, so it stays `parsed` like the vault).
