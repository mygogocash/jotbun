## Findings

### Blockers

None.

### Major

**M1. Every diary sync blanks the open insights screen, re-reads everything and resets it.** `insightsData.ts:96` puts `diaryView.outbox` in `useLoad`'s dependencies, so each new outbox array runs a full, non-quiet reload. The real diary emits a new array after every sync run (`spendDiary.ts:173-181`: `onOutcome` calls `refresh`, which reads the outbox again), and the scheduler runs every 60 s in the foreground (`sync/scheduler.ts:26`), on every save and on every live poke. Probe A (fake diary `set({ outbox: [] })`, same contents): the calendar tab unmounts (loading), reads go from 7 summaries, 1 history and 1 categories read to 14, 2 and 2, and the chosen day resets from "02 ตุลาคม 2569" to today. An open info tray closes the same way, since `SpendingTab` unmounts. Today's `InsightsScreen.tsx:97-112` loads on `[resources, month, today]`, reads drafts at render time, refreshes quietly when a draft resolves, and never shows another month's data (`loaded.data.month === month`). Fix: do the same, and add a test that a sync emit keeps the chosen day and makes no new reads.

**M2. The budget line is not on the bars' scale.** `MonthBars.tsx:42-50` puts the line at `budget / max(budget, largest bar)`, while the bars use `compareBars().permille`, which is relative to the largest month only (`recapModel.ts:198`). Whenever the budget is above every month (anyone under budget), the line sits at the top and the tallest bar nearly touches it. Probe C: ฿3,972 against a ฿18,000 budget draws the bar 105 of 110 units up to the line (95%) when it is 22%. The line's baseline is also 125 against the bars' 130. The plan asks for "the dashed `guideLine` at the budget". Fix: scale bars and line by the same ceiling from the same baseline. Test: the fixture's bar top sits well below the line.

**M3. Private mode marks the days with nothing spent (R65).** `CalendarTab.tsx:219-220` takes the border from the real step's paint even when `hidden`. `calendarPaint` draws a hairline only for step 0 (`walletPalette.ts:144-145`), so in private mode an empty day is an outlined box and a spending day is not. Probe D (light, private): day 3 has `border-width: 1px; border-color: rgba(230, 228, 238, 1.00)`, day 1 has `0px`. Because `raised` equals `ground` (`#F4F3F9`) and the calendar sits on the ground, the outline is the only shape left, so spending days stand out. The plan says "in private mode every day uses one neutral fill" (W10, D41), and the private test checks day 1's fill only. Fix: in private mode no border except today's 2 pt ink frame. Test: the private test compares an empty day with a spending day.

**M4. The early state labels every logged day with today's date, in raw digits.** `SpendingTab.tsx:203` writes `${today.slice(8)} ${month.slice(5)}` for each filled segment. Probe B (logged 1 and 2 Oct, today 24 Oct): both segments read "24 10". The plan's early state shows "the logged days", and `es-empty-insights` draws them as "ศ. 23 ต.ค." and "ส. 24 ต.ค." (the Global Constraint's dates through `src/ui/dateStep.ts`). This is the first chart a new person sees. Fix: label `cal.days.filter((d) => d.logged)` through `dateStep`. Test: the two logged days by date.

**M5. The calendar's money is neither exact nor formatted by `formatMinor` (hard rule, Global Constraint "Money", W9).** `CalendarTab.tsx:107` writes the month line as `฿${Math.round(Number(spentMinor) / 100).toLocaleString()}`, and `:213-214` uses the same for each day. Probe H: a month of ฿3,972.50 reads "ใช้ไป ฿3,973", and a ฿3,240.50 day reads "3,241". `toLocaleString()` with no locale also follows the phone's region (a `vi-VN` phone gets "3.240"). The protocol says amounts stay exact. The plan says satang go "through BigInt only, never a JS number; `formatMinor` stays the formatter underneath". W9's compact form keeps satang that are not zero. `walletMoneyText` and `perDayText` (`wallet/money/walletMoney.ts`) exist for exactly this. Fix: the month line with `walletMoneyText`, the cells in whole baht by BigInt, and a test with satang.

**M6. A compare month that fails to load is drawn and spoken as ฿0, with nothing said (checklist 2).** `insightsData.ts:67-80` catches each compare `summary()` failure and returns a zero summary cast `as unknown as MonthSummary`. Probe F (September holds ฿5,000; its read fails): the screen is ready, the chart says "กันยายน 2569 ฿0.00", and no problem is shown. A zero earlier month also turns on `insights.noPrevious` (`SpendingTab.tsx:120-122`). `RecapScreen.tsx:63-81` lets the same reads fail the load and tells it with Try again, and the planner's P2 says every load failure goes through `recapProblem`. Fix: let it fail (and reuse the month's own summary for its bar, as the recap does). Test: a failing compare month shows the problem with Try again, never ฿0.

**M7. The calendar hard-codes Thai, so English (and Vietnamese, which shows English) sees Thai.** In `CalendarTab.tsx`, `WEEKDAYS` (line 23), `'วันนี้'` (lines 223 and 321), `'บาท\nต่อวัน'` (line 277) and `'รายการ'` (line 367) sit outside the draft table, and the draft key `insights.today` is never used. Probe G (en): the tab shows "อาจอพพฤศส", "บาท", "ต่อวัน" and "วันนี้", and today's button says "24 October 2026 วันนี้". The Global Constraint ("E2") puts every new line in `wallet/insights/copy.ts` until P2-T38 moves the tables, so these would escape that move. `weekdayInitials(locale)` already exists in `ui/dateStep.ts:199` ("Column heads for a Sunday-first calendar grid"). Fix: use it and the draft table. Test: the English calendar holds no Thai.

**M8. The suite leaves at least 15 guards unheld (protocol step 5, checklist 10), and the analytics test is not the plan's.** I ran my own 15 mutants (`scratch/p2-t31-review/own_mutants.py`, logs in `own-mutant-logs/`) against `WalletInsights.render.test.tsx`. All 15 survived with 11/11 passing:
- R1: `insights_viewed` on every render (dependency array dropped).
- R2: the chart's spoken label drops this month's amount. `/3,972|12,480|0/` at test line 146 accepts any "0".
- R3: five bars drawn, not six; "draws six bars" never counts them.
- **R4, R5, R6 (R65):** the kept line, the budget label and the calendar's month line show their amounts in private mode.
- R7: next month is never disabled.
- R8: `insights.noPrevious` is never shown.
- R9: a load problem offers no Try again. No test covers any error path.
- R10: only the month's first history page is read (P4).
- **R11 (R65):** private-mode day numbers keep the step's colour.
- R12: every category says "1 รายการ".
- R13: the days-left line always says 0.
- R14: three logged days still count as early.
- R15: money in is drawn in ink.

The receipt's P10 ("every guard in the implementation has a dedicated mutant") does not hold. The Global Constraint "Analytics" asks tests to install `recordAnalytics()`, assert exactly one event per action, and check `JSON.stringify(sent)` for amounts. The test mocks the emitter with `vi.fn` and uses `toHaveBeenCalledWith`, so R1 survives. `PrivateMode.render.test.tsx` shows the expected form. Several plan cases are weaker than their names: "six bars", "each month's amount", "one neutral fill" (checked on day 1 only, so M3 survives), and violet money in (only the "+" glyph is checked). The plan's "both modes and languages" holds for one private case only. Fix: a case per survivor, with `recordAnalytics()`.

### Minor

**m1. The offline banner never shows.** `WalletInsightsScreen.tsx:127` tests `load.error.message.includes('network')`, but a `V1Error`'s message is `"NETWORK_ERROR (0)"` or `"TIMEOUT (0)"` (`api/client.ts:117`, `:304-322`). Probe E: the Thai message and ลองอีกครั้ง show, but the banner never does, so the planner's P2 ("displays SyncBanner when offline") does not hold. The problem's title is dropped too: `RecapScreen` shows it as a heading. `refreshSuggested` from `readAllPages` is read and never offered. Fix: as `InsightsScreen` does, `error instanceof V1Error && error.outcome === 'network'`, only where a diary is open.

**m2. The days-left line is one short of the board.** `SpendingTab.tsx:46-47` computes `totalDays - today`. Probe I: on 24 Oct it reads "อีก 7 วันจบเดือน", while `bw-insights` (the same 24 Oct as `es-empty-insights` and `wc-calendar`) reads "อีก 8 วันจบเดือน", counting today as W9 does. A past month, reached from the calendar's month step, reads "อีก 0 วันจบเดือน".

**m3. Day buttons do not tell VoiceOver their money out.** `CalendarTab.tsx:222-224` names each day by date alone. Probe J: day 1 shows "3,240" but says "1 ตุลาคม 2569". `calendarModel` already builds each day's spoken label with its money out and drafts (`insightsModel.ts:164-173`), but the tab passes `fullDate: (date) => date` and ignores it. Use `day.label` with `fullDateLabel`, and the date alone in private mode.

**m4. The in-and-out chart is an unnamed image.** `InOutTab.tsx:111-114` is `accessible` with `role="img"` and no label (probe K: `aria-label` null).

**m5. Built-in categories lose their names on the chosen day.** The server and the fakes send built-ins with `label: null` (`fakeResources.ts:40`). `CalendarTab.tsx:352`, `:367` and `:370` use `label` and `builtin_key` directly. Probe L: a Food record with no note reads "รายการ฿132", with no category. Use `categoryName(category, tc)` (`viewModels.ts:25`). The board's second line is "category · wallet", and the wallet is missing. The test and catalogue fixtures give built-ins a label, which hides this.

**m6. The catalogue draws the tabs on hand-built data, not the screen on fakes (checklist 3, in part).** `insightsSections.tsx` renders `SpendingTab`, `InOutTab` and `CalendarTab` over a literal `InsightsData`, so no E2 capture shows the title, the tab bar, the month header or the load states. P2-T20 instead draws `WalletsListScreen` through `screenSection` with `createFakeResources`. Also: no section shows the private calendar (R65's main surface), `bw-insights-large` is a copy of `bw-insights` with no large-case data, and `bw-insights-early` shows M4's "24 10".

**m7. Dates are not drawn through `dateStep` as the boards draw them.** The chosen-day heading is `${selectedDay.slice(8)} ${monthName}` ("02 ตุลาคม 2569"), where `wc-calendar` shows "เสาร์ 24 ต.ค. · วันนี้". Bar labels are `monthLabel(...).slice(0, 4)` ("พฤษภ", "กรกฎ", "Augu", "Octo"), where `bw-insights` shows "พ.ค. … ต.ค.". Both are in `MonthBars.tsx:130` and `InOutTab.tsx:177`.

**m8. Category rows invent and fork numbers.** `SpendingTab.tsx:240` defaults a count to 1. `:90-106` recomputes category totals in the screen when `summary.categories` is empty, which is always the case with the fake summary (`fakeResources.ts:255`). So the tests only ever exercise the fallback, and the production path, `summary.categories` through `topCategories`, is never rendered.

**m9. The kept line in In and Out shows a bare amount.** `keptOf`'s kept text is the amount alone in the exact form (`recapModel.ts:129`), so the banner reads "฿22,520.00" with no word for kept. In private mode the whole line becomes "฿••••", losing the short and even wording (W10 masks the amount, not the sentence).

**m10. The budget label uses the exact form.** `MonthBars.tsx:77` (`formatMinor`) reads "งบ ฿18,000.00", where `bw-insights` shows "งบ ฿18,000" (`walletMoneyText`, W9).

**m11. Calendar cells fall below 44 pt on a 375 pt phone, and the calendar has no plan for the largest text sizes.** The cells are `(375 - 36 - 36) / 7 = 43.3` pt wide. At AX5 the 10 pt amounts scale up to body's `maxScale` inside fixed 7-column cells. No test or catalogue section covers the calendar at AX5. The text also overrides `WalletText`'s role sizes inline, without roles.
