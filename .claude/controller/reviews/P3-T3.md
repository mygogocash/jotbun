### Does the 503 before the token reveal anything about a person? No.

- The gate reads only `config:v1`, one global KV entry, and the route table. Nothing per account, per token or per device decides it.
- **Probe A** (`zz-review-probe.test.ts`, passed). With `ai` paused, each of the four routes was sent six ways:
  - no client header and no token;
  - a garbage bearer token;
  - a token signed by an unpublished key;
  - a valid token for a stranger who never accepted the terms;
  - a member's token from a client below the minimum (`ios/0.0.1`);
  - a member who consented to AI.
- **Result:** for every route, the six answers are identical in status, in every header but `x-request-id`, and in the body but `request_id`: 503, `Retry-After: 120`, `{ code: 'AI_PAUSED', message_key: 'errors.ai_paused', retry_after_seconds: 120 }`.
- So the answer says nothing about whether a token is valid, whether an account exists, has accepted the terms or has consented to AI, or whether the app is current. It says only that AI is paused, which is the service's state. The API's gates already answer before authentication the same way (`feature-gates.ts`, its header comment).
- It confirms that the four paths exist, which their 426 and 401 answers already showed before this task.
