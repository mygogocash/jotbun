### Nit

**n1. The contract's comment on `AI_ROUTE_DEADLINE_MS` promises more than the code does.**
- `packages/contracts/src/v1/ai.ts:33-38` says "every vault step and model call of one request ends inside it".
- Steps are counted against the deadline, but never cut:
  - `step()` (`service.ts:62-66`) waits for the vault with no limit.
  - Only `tracedSend` sizes a call from the time left.
  - So a step after the last call, or a step slower than the deadline, ends past the deadline.
- Harmless in this task: only Ask Bun runs a step after a call, and its 25 s child ends 30 s inside its parent.
- From P3-T7 on, the refund step runs after a timed-out call on every route. Those answers will land one step past the deadline, inside the phone's 5 s.
- **Fix:** say that the deadline bounds the model calls, and that the steps count against it.

**n2. On parse, slip and note, the effort passed to `haikuRequest` is overwritten.**
- At `service.ts:118-119`, `:143-144` and `:188-189`, the explicit `output_config: { effort, format }` replaces the base's whole `output_config`.
  - So the effort passed to `haikuRequest` does nothing on those three routes.
  - Only Ask Bun (`:223`) uses the base's.
- So a change to `haikuRequest`'s effort on one of those routes does nothing, and no test fails. The routing case pins the effort actually sent.
- The pattern predates this task (`requestBase` had it).
- **Fix:** spread the base's `output_config` and add `format`, or give the request bases a `format` parameter. P3-T8 builds the slip's Haiku and Opus requests the same way, so it is the natural place for the fix.
