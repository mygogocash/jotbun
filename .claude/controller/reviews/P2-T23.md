### Minor

**m1. The front card's "today" leaves out a waiting draft.**

The build follows P2-T23's interface line here. But rh-offline, P2-T12's card and P2-T16's "today's money out" all count the draft. The brief cites rh-offline for this model and records no departure from it.

- **The code and its source:**
  - `homeWallets.ts:64-72` adds up only the expenses in `todayRecords`.
  - The brief's P7 (`briefs/P2-T23.md:84`) says "a draft is saving, not spent".
  - Plan line 1240 says "`todayOutMinor` sums today's confirmed expenses from it". The build follows this line exactly.
- **The other side:**
  - **rh-offline's front card** (`rh-offline.dc.html:61` and `:71`) shows ฿24,810, which is bw-home's ฿24,860 less the waiting ฿50. It reads "รวม ฿50 ที่กำลังบันทึก" and "วันนี้ −฿97", and its label says "วันนี้ใช้ไป 97 บาท". bw-home's same card on the same day says "วันนี้ − ฿47" (`bw-home.dc.html:71`). So the board's ฿97 is ฿47 confirmed plus the ฿50 draft.
  - **P2-T12's card spec** (plan line 796) gives this card's label as `บัญชีหลัก, เหลือ 24,810 บาท, รวม 50 บาทที่กำลังบันทึก, วันนี้ใช้ 97 บาท`. Its fixture is `{ balanceMinor: '2481000', pendingMinor: '5000', todayOutMinor: '9700' }` (`WalletCard.render.test.tsx:56`).
  - **P2-T16's `todayOutMinor`** (plan line 1030) is the toast's "today's money out" (W31). The plan defines it as "the confirmed total plus open expense drafts dated today whose id is not confirmed", and its test is `today's money out adds waiting drafts once` (line 1043).
  - **The brief contradicts itself on the same board.** P8 argues from rh-offline that the waiting ฿50 is in the month's figure: ฿12,530, "which only the estimate gives". P7 says the same ฿50 is not spent today.
- **Probe** (`tree/apps/app/src/wallet/home/zzprobe/rhOffline.probe.test.ts`, log `probe-rh-offline.log`). I fed rh-offline's data into `homeWallets`: ฿24,860 on the server, ฿47 confirmed today, and a waiting ฿50 expense dated today. It gives `{ balanceMinor: '2481000', pendingMinor: '5000', todayOutMinor: '4700' }`. The board and P2-T12's fixture say `'9700'`.
- **Effect:** once P2-T24 draws the stack, the offline Home's front card will say −฿47, and VoiceOver will say 47 บาท. The approved board says −฿97, and the budget card above it already counts the ฿50 (฿12,530). Nobody sees this before P2-T24, and the `wallet` area stays off until P2-T38.
- **Why minor:** the build follows the plan's own line for this function, and every figure it gives is exact. The plan disagrees with itself and with its board, and the protocol sends that question to the controller.
- **Ask:** a ruling before P2-T24, which edits `homeWallets.ts` anyway (the `frontFirst` swap). Two options:
  - **Keep confirmed only.** Record the departure from rh-offline (and from P2-T12's example) in this receipt and in P2-T24's brief.
  - **Count today's drafts in `spentByWallet`.** Then `today's spending counts confirmed expenses only` (`homeWallets.test.ts:91-108`) changes with it, and H17 becomes the guard the other way round. Choose which drafts:
    - only the waiting drafts the card already says are saving, which can never be counted twice because they were never sent; or
    - P2-T16's open drafts that are not yet confirmed, which needs the confirmed ids, as `unconfirmedDrafts` uses them for the pace.

**n2. The hook's test does not pin that a refused draft reads nothing (my R14 survives).**

- `homeData.ts:43` keeps every draft that is not acknowledged open. A draft that moves to needs_review is therefore not "resolved", and nothing is read.
- The mutant that counts needs_review as resolved passes both the hook's test and Home's suite (`own-mutants.out`, `own-mutants-home.out`). Its effect is one extra read in place each time the server refuses a draft.
- The base had the same gap and the extra read is harmless, so nothing needs fixing here. One more step in the draft case (move the waiting draft to `needs_review`, then expect no read) would close it, and would match that case's own claim.

**n3. The floating-point source scan no longer covers `availableBalances`.**

- `entrySave.render.test.tsx:266-272` scans `entryHelpers.ts`, `entryReferences.ts` and `entrySave.ts` for `parseFloat|parseInt|toFixed|Number(|Math.`.
- After the move, the function's body is in `spend/v1/redesign/availableBalances.ts`, which no scan reads.
- The 2^53 case kills B6 (the balance worked out in a JS number), so the behavioural guard is stronger than the scan was. This is a note for P5-T18b, which edits that function next.

**n4. The English drafts compose "Spent this month Amount hidden, Over budget".**

- `budgetCardWords.test.ts:75` pins it. `common.amountHidden`'s English starts with a capital letter, and it is dropped mid-sentence into `home.spokenSpent`.
