## Verdict

**No blocker and no major. Two minors and three nits.** The controller's questions, answered (details under "What holds"):

- **The copy route (P13): holds.** Every `/spend/*` route, the record screen included, renders inside the shell's host; in the wallet area `/spend/add` opens the shell's tray and never draws Quick add. Probed from the record screen with private mode on: no digit of the copied amount reaches any text, label or input. After #456 merges, its record screen only calls `useAddTray()`, so option (a) holds too.
- **One host (P14):** PROBE-C1 and C3 are shell cases and hold (X1 and X7 killed in my sample). No route or catalogue section mounts a second host inside the shell today. But the Node check would miss a route that wraps its screen in a second `WalletShell` (n1), and at runtime Settings' diary links mount a second spend layout, so the app can hold two shells, two hosts and two looks providers (m1).
- **The layout (P5, P6): holds.** The wallet branch sits after the sign-in redirect, the legacy build and `ask`, before the core branch. Nothing outside the tests relied on `all` meaning the core look.
- **Private mode (P16): holds.** The shell draws no amount and quotes nothing; what it mounts sits under `WalletRoot`'s provider.
- **Lifetimes (P8):** each provider is mounted once per spend layout, for its life; m1 is the exception.
- **Magic Tap and the + (P11, P12): hold.** 60 pt, named, hinted, a `button`; a screen's own Magic Tap answers first; Magic Tap cannot open a tray behind the lock. One look guard is unpinned (n3).
- **The inset (P10): holds** (84, 96, 0). Insights' overlap is 18 pt only with a 34 pt inset, 52 pt without (n2).
- **Two minors:** m1 (two spend layouts: stale looks under Settings, and a reorder that can undo one made there) and m2 (the notice's lookups and Thai naming are unpinned in the shell's suite; a one-line fix is proved).

### Minor

**m1. Settings' diary links mount a second spend layout, so "one host for the app's life" and the looks provider's lifetime hold per layout, not per app. A look, order or icon changed through Settings is not seen by Home's layout below it, and a reorder made there can undo it (P7, P8, P14; followups 359, 414, 777).**
- **How two layouts arise.**
  - The bar's ฉัน pushes `/settings` over the spend layout (`WalletTabBar.tsx:71`).
  - Settings lists Wallets, Categories and Budget while `planning` is on, and Reminders while `insights` is on; both are approved (`settingsModel.ts:54-60`, `featureFlags.ts:54`). Each row is `router.push('/spend/...')` (`SettingsScreen.tsx:171`; `SETTINGS_HREFS` at `settingsModel.ts:44-51`).
  - The root `Stack` gives `spend` no `getId` (`app/_layout.tsx:49`). expo-router 58's StackRouter appends a new route for a push, and for a navigate to a route that is not the focused one (`node_modules/expo-router/build/react-navigation/routers/StackRouter.js:253-344`).
  - `probes/stackPush.cjs`, on that router: from `[spend, settings]`, both a push and a navigate to `spend/wallets` give `[spend, settings, spend]`.
  - The stack keeps the lower screen mounted, so two spend layouts are mounted, each with its own shell: its own `AddTrayHost`, `SavedToastHost`, `CardFramesProvider` and `WalletLooksProvider`.
  - `DeviceDiaryProvider.tsx:125-130` names this very case ("The spend and Settings route groups are mounted together") and shares one diary module-wide for it. The looks have no such share.
- **What it does.** `WalletLooksProvider` reads the account store once per account at mount and keeps what it read in state (`WalletLooksProvider.tsx:119-129`). A change made in the upper layout is stored and shown there; the lower layout never reads it.
  - `zzReviewTwoShells`, case 1 (two shells side by side, as the Stack holds them): after the upper layout's `setOrder([Card, Main, Cash])`, the store holds it and the upper provider shows it, but the lower provider's order is still `[]`. The two `useAddTray()` values are different hosts.
  - Case 2: the lower layout's list, drawn from that stale order, moves Cash up one (`ReorderTray.tsx:54-61` computes the next order from the provider's), and the store becomes `[Cash, Main, Card]`. The move of Card to the top, made through Settings, is gone.
  - For the person, once P2-T24's Home draws the card stack from the looks: Home, ฉัน, Wallets (or Categories), a reorder, a new look or a category icon, back twice, and Home still shows the old order, looks or icons until the app restarts. At this base the wallet area's Home is still today's, so for now it shows in the lower layout's wallet list and in the add tray's category icons.
  - The second host is harmless in practice (each layout's tray covers the screen), but "one AddTrayHost for the app's life" does not hold as written.
- **Why minor, not major.** The wallet area is off until P2-T38. Nothing stored is wrong (each change reads the store before it writes); only what the lower layout shows is stale, apart from case 2's later reorder. There is no money or privacy effect. And the cause is the plan's placement of the provider in a per-layout shell meeting Settings' existing links, which this task's files cannot change: P2-T18 built what the plan says.
- **What to do.**
  - This task: correct the receipt's "The shell is mounted once, in the spend layout, so the looks provider lives as long as the spend area stays mounted" (`receipts/P2-T18.md:10`; the brief's P7 and P8 say the same). It is mounted once per spend layout, and Settings' diary links mount a second.
  - A carry, owned by the controller, settled before P2-T24 lands Home's card stack and at the latest before P2-T38. Either:
    - share the looks between providers: one store per account with subscribers, as `DeviceDiaryProvider` shares its diary (`WalletLooksProvider.tsx` is P2-T11's file). This keeps today's navigation and fixes the looks wherever they are read; or
    - keep one spend layout: Settings' diary links `router.dismissTo` into the existing layout, or a singular `spend` screen in the root Stack. Each changes what Back does, so it is a call for the owner.

**m2. The shell's notice wiring is unpinned: the chip could lose its category, or its Thai, and all 11 shell cases pass (P7, W31).**

The shell's chip case checks only `toContain('−฿50')` (`WalletShell.render.test.tsx:336`), and no other case reads the notice's title.
