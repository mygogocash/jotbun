## Verdict

Needs fixes: one major, five minors, four nits.

- **Major:** the pinned footer split (P3a, P10) leaves the tray's scrolling middle little or no room. At AX5 that hides the amount on a 667 pt phone, and a problem line pushes Next off the screen. At the default text size the same thing happens to income's name field while the system keyboard is up. The controller's focus 2 asked whether Next and the problem line stay reachable at AX5. By React Native's own arithmetic, they do not.
- **What holds:**
  - The flow's rules are right and exact.
  - Nothing confirmable is wrong: Next never takes an empty, zero or over-cap amount.
  - Private mode leaks no stored amount on the step. `add-amount` is unmasked by the controller's ruling.
  - Screen readers meet the parts in a sensible order.
  - `index.ts` is the planner's proven merge.
  - Every planner mutant I sampled is killed by an assertion.

## Findings

### Blocker

### Major

**M1. The pinned footer leaves the scrolling middle no room: at AX5 the amount is hidden and Next can leave the screen; at any size income's name is hidden while typed.**

Where:
- `Tray.tsx:292-312` (P2-T13's tray, not changed here): the header row and the `tray-footer` View are plain Views around the `ScrollView`. React Native's flex items do not shrink by default, so only the `ScrollView` (`flexShrink: 1`) gives way.
- This task puts three things in those pinned parts:
  - the type switch in the header, which stacks into three segments at large text (`TypeSwitchHeader.tsx:37`, ruling P7);
  - the whole keypad (`WalletKeypad.tsx:65`, 56 pt keys at large text);
  - the problem line and Next, in the footer (`AmountStep.tsx:103-119`, ruling P3a).
- iOS scales a line's height by the same capped multiplier as its font: `lineHeight * min(maxFontSizeMultiplier, fontScale)` (`node_modules/react-native/.../RCTAttributedTextUtils.mm:135` and `:247`). So at AX5:
  - a label line is 23 × 3.12 = 71.8 pt;
  - the stacked switch is 251 pt;
  - the keypad is 236 pt;
  - Next is 105 pt;
  - the card's padding and gaps are 60 pt.

The budget probe (`scratch/p2-t14-review/ax5-budget.ts`, output in `ax5-budget.log`) imports the real type scale. It gives the middle's height, in Thai at AX5, after the switch, the keypad and Next. The amount's one line at AX5 needs 84 pt.

- **How many problem lines.** My estimate at 46.8 pt in a roughly 340 pt column (glyph widths assumed, not measured):
  - zero's กรอกจำนวนเงินที่มากกว่าศูนย์ is about 2 lines;
  - the cap's จำนวนเงินสูงสุด ฿999,999,999.99 is about 3 lines, since the number breaks.
- **The cap case.** The test keeps Next enabled there (`AmountStep.render.test.tsx:217-219`). So at AX5 on the board's own phone, the one button that would go on sits about 91 pt below the screen until the person deletes a digit.
- **English.** The SE's middle is 12 pt; one problem line puts Next 50 of 96 pt off the screen.
- **The keyboard, at the default text size.**
  - With the system keyboard up for income's name field (`AmountStep.tsx:96`, `:230-237`), the switch row (52 pt), the 46 pt keypad and Next (262 pt) all stay pinned.
  - That leaves the middle 5 pt on the SE, 10 pt on the mini, and 41 to 45 pt on 6.1-inch phones.
  - The name row is 58 pt. The keyboard heights are standard iOS values with the predictive bar (260 / 336 / 346 pt), and are assumptions.
  - The brief recorded this case as a SKIPPED device check. The numbers say it fails on small phones.
- **The board.** ax-large-tray draws a different header at AX5: a one-line 55 pt title (66 pt), the amount pinned under it, and a 274 pt middle. The stacked switch alone takes about 185 pt the board does not spend.
- **Plan clauses:**
  - the Global Constraints' accessibility line 87 ("Dynamic Type to AX5 ... with nothing truncated");
  - W30;
  - Review Focus 3 (line 116: "... in the tray (P2-T11, T12, T14)");
  - the task's own binding (line 926: "the amount stays above the scrolling middle"). The binding's deviation was accepted in P10 on the premise that the middle has room.
- **Why no test fails.** happy-dom has no layout, so no render test can see this. E2-B's AX5 capture is by hand and on one phone, and no screenshot opens a keyboard.

Ask, for the controller to rule before the fix round. The cheapest fixes stay in this task's files:
1. At large text, take the switch out of the pinned header row: render it at the top of the scrolling middle and leave the title in the header, as ax-large-tray draws. Another way is one row with the segment labels' scale capped so three fit.
2. Keep the problem line from growing the pinned footer at AX5: cap its scale, or show it in the middle's end at large text.
3. While income's name field is focused (or the system keyboard is up), have `AmountFooter` hide `WalletKeypad` and keep Next, so the middle gets the keypad's 208 pt back.
4. The alternative is the tray's own (`Tray.tsx`: a footer that scrolls when it does not fit, or a pinned slot under the header), which is outside this task's files.

Whichever is chosen, capture `bw-tray-amount` at AX5 on a 667 pt phone and `bw-tray-income` with the keyboard up before the wallet area ships, not only at E2-B.

### Minor

**m1. Income's name field is not 44 pt in its own box.**
- `AmountStep.tsx:230-237`: the `TextInput` has `padding: 0` and no `minHeight`, so its box is one 17 pt line in a 58 pt row. The caption and the tile beside it do not focus it.
- Probe R1 (`scratch/p2-t14-review/probes.log`): `{"minHeight":"","padding":"0px","fontSize":"17px"}`, which fails `minHeight >= 44`.
- The plan's line 87 says "controls at least 44 × 44 pt in their own box ... and the tests measure the box". Every other `TextInput` in the app sets `minHeight: BUN_TOUCH` (`NoteField.tsx:77`, `SearchField.tsx:51`, `WalletParts.tsx:249`).
- The 44 pt sweep (`AmountStep.render.test.tsx:333-353`) mounts spending, so it never meets the field.
- **Fix:** `minHeight: 44` on the input (trim the row's vertical padding to keep 58 pt), and add the field to the sweep with an income mount.

**m2. A transfer's amount still shows the quick chips.**
- `AmountStep.tsx:97` renders `<QuickChips chips={chips} />` for every type.
- Probe R9 (`probe-r9.log`): switched to โอน, the chip กาแฟเย็น 65 บาท is still there.
- Ruling P4h says a transfer's amount "stands alone". Today the often-logged amounts live only in the Tell Bun sheet, which Quick add never offers for a transfer (`QuickAddScreen.tsx:262`, `:335-342`). P2-T16 builds the chips from often-logged spending, so a tap would put a spending amount on a transfer.
- The transfer case (`AmountStep.render.test.tsx:258-269`) mounts with no chips, so it cannot see this.
- **Fix:** `type === 'transfer' ? null : <QuickChips ... />`, with chips in the transfer case. The second review's m4 found the same.

**m3. A locked tray still lets the type switch change the entry's type.**
- Probe R6: a `Tray` with `locked`; close says `aria-disabled="true"`, but a tap on รายรับ renames the dialog จดรายรับ and plays `segmentChange`.
- `TypeSwitchHeader` has no `disabled` (ruling P7: "the plan's props have none"). Today's `TypeSwitch` has one, and Quick add passes `disabled={locked}` (`TypeSwitch.tsx:21`, `QuickAddScreen.tsx:257`).
- P2-T16 keeps the switch as the header on every step and locks the tray while it saves (plan lines 991 and 994). So during a save the type can change under the in-flight draft, and an unconfirmed save's Try again could resend another type.
- **Fix:** add `disabled?: boolean` now (dims, no tick, no `onType`), as `WalletKeypad` has for the locked tray, or make it a stated must for P2-T16.

**m4. After a satang fill, a keypad with no decimal key says "use at most two decimal places".**
- Probe R7: a keypad opened on `6550` (a chip, Bun's fill, or a copy, since `duplicateDraft` keeps `amount_minor`, `recordModel.ts:156`) refuses any digit with ใช้ทศนิยมไม่เกินสองตำแหน่ง.
- Two deletes later it shows `฿65.`, a point the keypad cannot type, with Next enabled. The amount stays exact (฿65.00), so this is wording and display, not money.
- `amountProblem` passes `too_precise` through (`addFlow.ts:57`). The brief and the catalogue plan only for `zero` and `too_large` (`addSections.tsx:51-54`: "today's two reachable lines"). No case covers `too_precise`.
- **Ask:** a ruling for P2-T16, the owner of the fills. Either drop the satang when the first digit is typed after a fill, or say something this keypad can act on. Then add a case. The second review's m3 found the same.

**m5. Untested guards: my mutants survive.**
- Five of my own mutants pass all 12 render cases (`scratch/p2-t14-review/own-mutants.out`, logs in `own-mutant-logs/`). Each needs one assertion:
  - **M1.** The typed amount leaves its polite live region (`AmountStep.tsx:75`; ruling P4c). Probe R3 shows the region is there today (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`).
  - **M2.** `useAnnouncement(problem)` is removed (`AmountStep.tsx:106`; P6, iOS). It is native-only, so R72's rule applies: pin it with a Node test that reads the source.
  - **M3.** Income's typed amount is drawn violet (`AmountStep.tsx:86`). Ruling P4d and the colour constraint say ink; probe R2 shows ink today, but only spending's colour is asserted.
  - **M5.** The name field's `maxFontSizeMultiplier` is removed (`AmountStep.tsx:235`, W30). No case mounts income at AX5.
  - **M6.** A wallet id no longer listed is shown as `จาก<id>` (`AmountStep.tsx:128`). Ruling P4e says "not chosen or not listed" gives `add.chooseWallet`; only `null` is tested, and a stale id is reachable when `duplicateDraft` runs without the open list (`recordModel.ts:152`).
- M4 (income's amount with a plus) is killed by the sections case.

## What holds (evidence)

**The flow (focus 1).**
- `stepAfterAmount`: a transfer goes to `transfer`, everything else to `confirm`.
- `amountProblem`: a refused key's problem, else `zero`, and nothing for an empty keypad (the brief's anti-nag reading of the plan's line 926).
- `moveAllMinor`: null for null or unknown wallets, `'0'` and below zero (F18 rerun, killed).
- `balancesAfter`: BigInt, past 2^53, unknown stays unknown, one wallet on both sides keeps its balance (F22 rerun, killed).
- `confirmRows`: built-in names come through `tc` (P3e; a `WalletT` would leave Food in English). The note is trimmed, and the day is `dayLabel`'s.

**Nothing confirmable is wrong.**
- Next is disabled exactly while `evaluateKeypad(keypad).ok` is false: empty and zero are disabled (A1 rerun, killed), and a tenth digit is refused with the amount kept.
- A satang fill evaluates exactly (m4 is wording only).
- "Over the balance" is not a rule of this step; wallets may go below zero, and Quick add does not block it either.

**Unknown balances** stay `null` in both functions.

**AddStep `transfer` (focus 3)** matches P2-T16's line 992. A transfer's Next goes to `transfer` (probe and A3). See the notes below for what the order means for P2-T15 and P2-T16.
