### Major

**M1. After an unconfirmed create, the new-wallet flow holds its only way out. When the resend is then blocked, nothing on the screen can be pressed.**
- **The code:**
  - `NewWalletFlow.tsx:120` counts `unconfirmed` as `locked`, and `:210` holds step 2's back (`disabled: locked`).
  - While unconfirmed, Try again is the only control (`:201`).
  - A resend can be blocked: `session_changed` or `upgrade_required` on a command still unknown (`walletCommands.ts:86`). Then Try again goes too, and เพิ่มกระเป๋า stays held (`:206`).
- **Probe PR2 fails:**
  - Setup: lose the create's response, then refuse the resend as `session_changed`.
  - Every button on the step is held: `กลับ=held | 1=held | … | ลบตัวเลข=held | เพิ่มกระเป๋า=held`. The name is read-only and the radios are held.
  - Pressing กลับ does nothing: no router call, and the step line still says `ขั้นที่ 2 จาก 3`.
  - With no block, Try again is the only enabled control, so a person who is offline cannot leave until the network returns.
- **Why it matters:**
  - In the wallet area, nothing else leaves this screen on iOS:
    - P2-T18's `walletTab` hides the tab bar on `/spend/wallet/x` (plan lines 1068 and 1081).
    - The spend layout is a `Slot`, which has no swipe back.
    - The flow registers no back handler (the receipt's own Risks). Only Android's system back escapes.
  - Today's form keeps Back enabled in every phase, unconfirmed and blocked included (`WalletsScreen.tsx:515`).
  - Until P2-T18 lands, R3's bar still shows under `/spend/wallet/*`, because `SpendTabShell`'s `BAR_HIDDEN` (`:26`) lacks `wallet`. That escape goes away with P2-T18.
- **Unpinned:** my mutant Y1 (step 2's back never held) survives all 12 NewWallet cases. No case says what back should do here.
- **The brief:** ruling P8 says back on step 2 "is held while a create is out". That fits a create in flight (Review Focus 1). It should not hold the screen once the outcome is unknown and only the person can act.
- **Fix:**
  - Hold step 2's back only while `saving`.
  - While `unconfirmed`, let back leave the flow (`leave()`, as step 1's back does), so the kinds cannot change the look under a pending command. The list reads again on return and shows the wallet if the create committed.
  - Add the case: after an unconfirmed create, and after a blocked resend, back leaves the flow and keeps no look.

### Minor

**m1. An edit's save that lands after the person pressed back goes back a second time.**
- **The code:**
  - The edit header's back stays enabled while a save is out (`WalletEditScreen.tsx:243`).
  - On `done`, `send` calls `onBack()` whatever happened meanwhile (`:171`).
  - An archive calls `onArchived()` (`router.dismissTo('/spend/wallets')`, `:170`) the same way.
- **Probe PR1 fails:**
  - Setup: hold `updateWallet`, rename the wallet, press บันทึก, then press กลับ.
  - The probe then unmounts the screen, as the `Slot` does on back, and releases the save.
  - Result: `router.back` is called twice (`expected 2 to be 1`).
- **Why it matters:**
  - On a slow network, a person who leaves while the save is out is taken back once more when it lands: from the wallet detail to the list, or from the list to Home.
  - Today's form never navigated after its save, because its stages are state inside one route.
  - The new-wallet flow avoids this, because its back is held while saving.
- **Fix:** do one of these, and pin it with PR1:
  - hold the header's back while `saving`, as the flow does;
  - or skip `onBack()` and `onArchived()` once the screen has unmounted (a mounted ref).

**m3. In the wallet area the tracking start can no longer be changed anywhere, so a new wallet can never hold an entry dated before the day it was made.**
- **The code:**
  - `WalletFields.tsx:205-207` shows เริ่มจด as text.
  - Ruling P9 says it is "shown, not changed here; today's Wallets screen keeps its date chip (P20)".
  - Today's screen, with its `DateChip` (`WalletsScreen.tsx:526`), is routed only from `app/spend/wallets.tsx`. That route shows the new list while the `wallet` area is on (`:22`).
- **The consequence:**
  - Records can be back-dated only to a wallet's tracking start. See spec line 595; the server refuses earlier dates with `DATE_BEFORE_TRACKING_START` (`parts.tsx:132`).
  - Every wallet the flow makes starts today. A person who sets up on the 24th (P2-T35's checklist opens this flow) cannot log the 20th to the 23rd in that wallet.
  - Nothing in the wallet area can move the date.
- **The plan and the board:** the plan's "the tracking start as `walletsModel` sets it" (line 1177) can be read either way. wc-wallet-edit draws it as a link (`wc-wallet-edit.dc.html:69`: `<a href="#">เริ่มจด 1 ต.ค. 2569</a>`).
- **Fix:**
  - This is the owner's call at E2-B: either make เริ่มจด open a date tray (with today's `validateTrackingStart` rules), or keep it as text and state the consequence.
  - Either way, the receipt's Risks should say it.
  - The E2-B note should not say that today's screen keeps the chip, since that screen cannot be reached in the wallet area.

### Nit

**n1. In the ceremony, the new card does not drop from "about one card's height above its place".**
- **The code:** `walletAddedEntering` now starts the card at `originY: -200` (`useWalletMotion.ts:73` and `:79`).
- **Reanimated's coordinates:** in 4.7.1, `SlideInUp` animates `originY` to `values.targetOriginY` (`src/layoutReanimation/defaultAnimations/Slide.ts:239-260`). That value is in the parent's coordinates: the values carry a separate `targetGlobalOriginY` (`src/animationBuilder.tsx:12-19`), and `SlideInDown`'s start, unlike `SlideInUp`'s, is an offset from `targetOriginY`.
- **The distance:** in the ceremony, the card sits below the close button in the scroll content (`NewWalletFlow.tsx:370-374`: `insets.top + 8`, then 44, then a gap of 16). So it travels about `insets.top + 268` pt (about 327 pt with a 59 pt inset), starting above the edge of the screen.
- **The overshoot:** a spring's overshoot scales with the distance, so expect about 70 pt rather than the receipt's 44.
- **Fix:** one line. Wrap the `Animated.View` in its own `View`, so its local origin is 0. Or keep the numbers and correct the E2-B note.

**n2. In private mode, the refused-archive line shows `฿0`.**
- **The code:** `WalletEditScreen.tsx:333` passes `walletMoneyText('0')`, while the archive line just below it masks its ฿0 (`WalletAmount minor="0"`).
- **Probe PR5:** the refused line reads `ยังไม่ได้เก็บ บัตรเก่า ถาวร: ยอดคงเหลือไม่ใช่ ฿0 แล้ว`, beside `เก็บได้เมื่อยอดเหลือ ฿•••• ตอนนี้มี ฿•••• …`.
- **Why it matters:** it reveals nothing of the person's money, but Review Focus 2's sweep (P2-T37) will count it.
- **Fix:** mask it as the line below does, or add it to the receipt's P2-T37 note.
