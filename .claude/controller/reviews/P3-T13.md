### Minor

**m1. A draft handed to `applyAiDraft` after the tray has closed, while it leaves, is taken by the hidden entry and never reviewed.**

- **Why:**
  - `applyAi` refuses only while `locked` (`AddTrayHost.tsx:421`).
  - `locked` covers saving, unconfirmed and conflict (`entrySave.ts:92`). It does not cover a hidden tray, and it does not cover `saved`.
  - The host's `applyAiDraft` (`AddTrayHost.tsx:217`) reaches the entry until the entry unmounts (`:224-228`). That can be up to `CLOSED_BOUND_MS`, 1.5 s, when iOS's dismissal does not end (`:144`).
  - The dropped review runs only when `visible` changes (`:372-374`), so a draft taken after that change has no review left to send.
- **Probes** (`probes-1.log`, `probes-2.log`; `dismissals.hold` keeps the entry until the bound):
  - **REVIEW-2 fails.** An AI draft, then close (one `dropped`, correct), then a second draft while the tray leaves. Once the entry has gone, the reviews are still `[dropped haiku]`: the second draft was never reviewed.
  - **REVIEW-2b fails.** The same after a save landed: `[kept haiku]` only. `status` is `saved`, which is not locked.
  - **REVIEW-2c passes, and shows the draft was taken.** A third late draft makes the hidden entry send `dropped` for the second (`[dropped haiku, dropped opus]`), and the third is then lost.
- **Against the plan:** "closing or discarding with an AI origin unsaved reviews `dropped` once". The entry is discarded while it holds an unsaved AI origin, and nothing is sent.
- **Reach:**
  - No caller exists today.
  - P3-T17's ใช้ข้อมูลนี้ and แก้ไข are presses on a visible tray, so they cannot reach it.
  - P3-T18's one-draft path calls `applyAiDraft` when the parse answers, which can be just after the person closed the tray.
  - The cost is analytics only: a draft the person never saw is either uncounted or counted late. No record, amount or consent is affected.
- **Fix:** one line and one case. Either:
  - the host's `applyAiDraft` returns while `!now.current.visible`; or
  - `applyAi` returns while `!visible`.

  Add a case like REVIEW-2 that expects the late draft to be refused (nothing filled, nothing reviewed). Or carry it to P3-T18 as a must (see Notes).

**m2. A save in flight when the host goes away still sends its review, and `record_saved`, when it lands (P7, R119).**

- **Why:**
  - `finish` calls the entry's `onSaved` (`entrySave.ts:110`) whether or not the entry is still mounted.
  - That `onSaved` takes the pending review and sends it (`AddTrayHost.tsx:341-347`).
  - `finish` then sends `record_saved` (`entrySave.ts:111-115`).
- **Probe REVIEW-1 fails.** An AI draft, Save online with `createTransaction` held, then `cleanup()`, so the host goes away as on a sign-out. Releasing the create then sends:
  - `ai_draft_reviewed {outcome: kept, fields_edited: 0, model: claude-haiku-5-5, escalated: false}`;
  - `record_saved {kind: expense, source: photo, category_key: travel, has_photo: false, offline: false, seconds_to_save: 0}`.
- **Against the brief:** P7 says a host that goes away "sends nothing: that review would land after the session changed, under the next person's consent". The receipt's "What changed" repeats "A host that goes away sends nothing".
  - Its Risks line ("sends no review for an unsaved AI draft") is accurate.
  - The broad claim is not.
- **Why only minor:**
  - The review describes a save that did commit.
  - `record_saved` has had the same exposure since 1D-T7. I found no review of 1D-T7, P2-T16 or P3-T5 that weighed it.
  - The host is mounted only by the catalogue today (`dev/catalogue/wallet/addHostSection.tsx:41`).
  - The window is a sign-out between Save and the save's answer. A device save answers in milliseconds, and an online one whose session was revoked fails without `finish`.
- **What to do:**
  - Correct the receipt's line to: "an unsaved draft's review is not sent; a save in flight still sends its review and `record_saved` when it lands".
  - Carry the question to the task that mounts the host in the app (P2-T18) and to 1D's follow-ups: should a `finish` that runs after its screen is gone send either event? Both would be held under R119 and sent on the next yes.

## The controller's focus, answered

**Each review is sent once (P7).** Yes on every path I could reach. There are no two reviews for one draft, and no `kept` or `edited` without a commit.

- **Saved:**
  - `onSaved` takes the review before it sends it (`AddTrayHost.tsx:343-346`).
  - `onSaved` runs only from `finish` (`entrySave.ts:110`). `finish` runs only after one of:
    - the device commit (`:194-195`);
    - the server's acknowledgement (`:123-126`);
    - a create the server already holds with the same details (`:132-138`).
  - REVIEW-3 sends two Save presses in one React batch (P2-T16's PROBE-4): 2 device writes and 2 `record_saved` (followups line 784, unchanged), but 1 review.
- **Dropped:**
  - On the close (`:372-374`), and on a replacement, by the sheet (`:415`) or a new AI draft (`:422`).
  - The clear (`:367`) keeps it once (REVIEW-2d).
- **The receipt's risk is accurate:**
  - An unconfirmed save the person closes is reviewed `dropped` once, with no `record_saved` (REVIEW-4).
  - A conflict closed with Done is reviewed `dropped` once (REVIEW-4b).
  - The two events agree: neither says "saved" for a save whose outcome is unknown.
  - The choice is unpinned (m3, M5).
- **Two exceptions:** m1 (a late draft taken by a leaving tray is never reviewed) and m2 (a save in flight sends its review after the host has gone).

**The source on the wire (P5, H9).** Holds.

- `buildQuickAdd` adds `source` only when one is given (`quickAddModel.ts:197`). The legacy Quick add passes none (`QuickAddScreen.tsx:214`), so its create has no `source` key (Q1 and E2, killed).
- "Last" is cosmetic (n1). A resend sends the first attempt's stored input, source and all (`entrySave.ts:169-171`), and while it is unconfirmed the origin cannot change (`AddTrayHost.tsx:421`).
- The device entry is `toPendingEntry(input)` (`quickAddModel.ts:201`, `:205-207`), so it carries the create's source.
- The host reads `origin.current.source` once, for the create and the track alike (`AddTrayHost.tsx:444-450`).
- The diary validates and keeps it (P3-T2: `diaryRepository.ts:151-156`, `diaryRepository.test.ts:119-133`), and the contracts take it on both creates (`transactions.ts:82`, `:99`).
- Quick chips and edits leave the origin:
  - the sheet case changes every guess and still saves `tell_bun` (kills H1);
  - REVIEW-8 shows a chip over an AI draft saves `photo` and is reviewed `edited` 1;
