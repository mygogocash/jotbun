  - `GET /v1/drafts` (`bound_session`): the open drafts, newest slip first (its date, then its time, then the draft's id, each descending; a slip with no date, or no time, after every one with it), `limit` 50 by default and at most 200 (the API's `parseLimit`), an opaque keyset `cursor` (base64url JSON of the last draft's date, time and id; anything else is 422 on `cursor`), `total` the count of every open draft, and each draft through P4-T3's `slipDraftDto` with `slipDuplicateOf` (the oldest live record with its amount and date).
  - `POST /v1/drafts/confirm` (`current_policy`, gates `imports` and `writes`, 200): one `slip_draft.confirm` command (entity `slip_inbox`, outcome `accepted`) for 1 to 50 drafts. Its headers, then the body (`parseSlipConfirmRequest`: distinct draft and record ids), then the command. The mutation reads the drafts with one JSON parameter: a draft that is gone (confirmed, dismissed or expired since the list was read) is 409 `VERSION_CONFLICT` and nothing is written. Each record goes through `createTransactionRow`, in the request's order, with the source the vault sets from the draft's origin (`share` gives `share_extension`, every other origin `slip_import`, A1) and the draft's slip reference; a `source` or `slip_ref_id` key in a record is 422 `UNKNOWN_FIELD`, and any refusal names its draft, `drafts[<i>].transaction.<field>`, with nothing written. The drafts go, their references become `confirmed` with their records, and the change group is each record's change, then the inbox's new count. A resend replays its acknowledgement. No `record_saved` is sent (S18).
  - `POST /v1/drafts/:id/dismiss` (`current_policy`, gate `imports`, 200): one `slip_draft.dismiss` command (outcome `updated`); the draft's id (404 when it is not a UUID), then an empty body; an unknown or closed draft is 404. The draft goes, its reference stays known as `dismissed`, and the group is the inbox's change.
  - The three drafts routes carry `release: 'SLIP_IMPORT_ENABLED'`: until it is on they answer exactly as a missing path does (S13).
  - `POST /v1/slips/refs` (`current_policy`, gate `imports`, 204, no release): a plain write (S1). A record that is not live is 404; an unknown hash becomes a `recorded` reference to the record, which takes it as its slip reference when it has none; a known hash is left as it is. The record's version, source and change log do not move (H9).
  - `pruneSlips` and `slipsDueAt` (`vault/slips/prune.ts`), the sixth of 1B-T11's file prunes (before the snapshots' prune): an open draft whose `expires_at` has come goes, its reference becomes `expired` (the slip stays known), and one inbox change is committed under `derivedUuid(vaultId, 'slip-prune', now)` with no receipt; nothing expired writes nothing. Backfill use rows go once more than 31 Bangkok days old. `slipsDueAt` is the earliest draft expiry or the Bangkok midnight that makes the oldest backfill row prunable; after a prune at it, the next is later or null (R37).
  - `createTransactionRow` (`vault/domain/transactions.ts`): `createTransaction`'s checks and its one insert path, for a record made inside another command (this task's confirm, then P4-T11's statement rows). `createTransaction` checks its body before its command, as before, and inserts through the same path; its answers and its digested payload are unchanged.
  - `appendChanges` takes `Pick<VaultContext, 'sql' | 'now' | 'registry'>`; `FilePrune` takes the file context with the vault's `registry`, which `UserVault` builds once per instance and `runFileJobs` passes to every prune.
