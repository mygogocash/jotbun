- **What changed:**
  - The tray (`Tray.tsx`, with the rule in `trayGesture.ts`): a transparent React Native `Modal` with `animationType="none"` and `statusBarTranslucent`, its content in a `GestureHandlerRootView`. The card sits 8 pt from the left, right and bottom (plus the bottom safe area), with radius 34, on `sheet`, with the boards' edge (a soft shadow by day, a 1 pt light line at night). At the largest text sizes (`useLargeText()`) it is full height under the top safe area. The backdrop is ink at 38% by day and black at 60% at night; it is decorative and never a tab stop (`tabIndex={-1}`). The header row holds back (when `onBack` is given), the title or `header`, and close. Back and close are 44 pt `raised` circles with 16 pt glyphs, and the title is an `h2`.
  - Names and focus (P8): on the web, the Modal's own layer is the one named dialog and takes focus when it opens. On a phone the card is the dialog and the title takes focus through `moveAccessibilityFocus`. With a `header`, an accessible header 1 by 1 pt takes the title's place at the start of the row, and the title still names the dialog. Under the lock nothing of the tray is named.
  - One close rule (P9): `requestClose()` serves close, the backdrop, `onAccessibilityEscape`, the Modal's `onRequestClose` and the swipe. It calls `onClose` only while `visible && !locked`. While locked, close is disabled (`aria-disabled`, `accessibilityState`, dimmed to 0.4).
  - Exit and `onClosed` (P10): the Modal stays shown while the card plays `trayExiting(reduced)`, and hides after `trayExiting(reduced).getDuration()` (220 ms, or 150 ms under Reduce Motion). `onClosed` fires once: on iOS from the Modal's `onDismiss`, elsewhere right after the hide. Opening the tray again while it leaves cancels the hide and says nothing.
  - Motion (P11): the tray reads `useWalletReducedMotion()` live. The card enters with `trayEntering(reduced)` and leaves with `trayExiting(reduced)`, and its height follows `stepLayout(reduced)`. A `step` change plays the `trayStep` haptic once through `playHaptic`, only while the tray is open. The content is keyed on `step` and enters with `stepEntering()`, and the first step's fade is skipped (`LayoutAnimationConfig skipEntering`). The swipe's spring back is `withSpring(0, { ...motion.spring.gentle, reduceMotion: ReduceMotion.Never })`, a jump under Reduce Motion (W6).
  - The swipe (P12): `usePanGesture({ activeOffsetY: 10, failOffsetX: [-20, 20], runOnJS: true, onUpdate, onDeactivate })` on the header row. The card follows the finger down only, closes past `shouldDismiss`, and otherwise springs back. Asked to close, it stays where the finger let go.
  - The lock (P13): while `useLockCover().hidden` is true, `LockCoverArt` takes the place of the backdrop and the card inside the Modal, and the content unmounts. The card's exit is skipped (`LayoutAnimationConfig skipExiting`), so it never shows over the cover.
  - The keyboard: the card sits in a `KeyboardAvoidingView` (`behavior` `padding` on iOS, as `EntryPage` does), and `footer` is pinned under the `ScrollView`, as `ActionFooter` does.
  - `PrimaryButton.tsx` (P14a): at least 54 pt (`minHeight`), radius 16, `primary` with `onPrimary` words in the `body` role at 700. Busy says `busyLabel` (or keeps `label`), is marked busy and ignores presses. Disabled is marked, ignores presses and dims to 0.4. A pressed button dims to 0.85.
