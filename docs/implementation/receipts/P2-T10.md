- **What changed:**
  - Money text (`walletMoney.ts`, pure, over `formatMinor`): `walletMoneyText` gives the compact form (`฿12,480`, with `.00` dropped only when the satang are zero) or the exact form (`exact`, always two decimals). An expense takes the true minus (U+2212), income a plus, and a balance a minus only below zero. `sign: 'none'` draws the size of the amount, so a negative's minus goes too. Zero is never signed. `perDayText` gives whole baht, rounded down. `spokenMoney` says `12,480 บาท`, `50 บาท 25 สตางค์` or `ติดลบ 3,920 บาท` through `common.baht`, `common.satang` and `common.minus`. Anything but a canonical satang string throws the `TypeError` that `formatMinor` throws. `HIDDEN_AMOUNT` is `฿••••`.
  - `WalletAmount`: DM Sans 700 (`LATIN_UI_FONT_FAMILY`) in every language. Income is violet unless a tone or a `color` says otherwise (`color` is the text colour of the material underneath). The label comes first, then the amount in words: a balance says its minus, any other sign says the size. Letter spacing is −0.02 em at 28 pt and above, with `maxFontSizeMultiplier={amountMaxScale(size)}` and never `numberOfLines`. In private mode it draws `HIDDEN_AMOUNT` at the amount's own size and line (the mask spaced 0.12 em, as bw-private draws it) in the amount's colour, and says only that the amount is hidden (`common.amountHidden`).
  - `IconButton`: 44 × 44 pt in its own box, a `card` fill with a 1 pt `hairline` ring, the caller's 24 pt glyph and `accessibilityLabel`. `disabled` sets `accessibilityState`, `aria-disabled`, the native `disabled` and 0.4 opacity.
  - Private mode: the device store (P2-T3's `walletDeviceStore()`, which the sign-out erase clears) holds `private-mode` (`'1'` when on) and `private-tip-seen`. `PrivateModeProvider` (with an optional `store`, P4) reads both names before it answers: until then `usePrivateMode()` says hidden, not ready, and the eye waits (P5). `toggle` (R61) writes or removes the name, announces `privacy.hiddenAnnounce` or `privacy.shownAnnounce` with `announcePolitely`, and calls `trackPrivateModeToggled()` once, with no properties. `dismissTip` stores `private-tip-seen`. Outside a provider, `usePrivateMode()` shows amounts and changes nothing.
  - `PrivateEyeButton` is an `IconButton` whose label says what the next tap does (`ซ่อนยอดเงิน` or `แสดงยอดเงิน`) and whose glyph is drawn open or shut. `PrivateTip` is a polite status region (`POLITE_LIVE_REGION`) on the palette's `toast` fill, shown while amounts are hidden and until the person closes it; its close is 44 pt.
  - `WalletRoot` wraps the children in `PrivateModeProvider` inside `WalletMotionProvider` (P2).
  - Copy: `privacy/copy.ts` holds the seven `privacy.*` lines as draft tables (`th` and `en`), read through `useDraftCopy`. The locale comes from `IntlContext` without `useIntl` (P7).
  - Catalogue: `WALLET_KIT_SECTIONS` holds `bw-amounts` (`Bun Wallet: amounts`) and `bw-private` (`Bun Wallet: private mode`), spread into `WALLET_SECTIONS`. `bw-private` mounts its own `PrivateModeProvider` over an in-memory store, hidden with the tip open, so the catalogue never reads or writes the phone's setting (P3).
